🔑 Simon's Key Preparation Tips

  1. Spend more time preparing than testing — You won't improve just by writing test essays. Study the topic first, build up your ideas, opinions and vocabulary.
  2. "Steal" his ideas — Don't just use vocabulary you already know. Practise linking Simon's topic ideas together to write "perfect essays".
  3. Use this book with the website — ielts-simon.com has lessons and videos showing you how to use the ideas from this book.
  4. The key to a high score — It's NOT grammar or linking words — it's topic language (words and phrases related to the question topic).
